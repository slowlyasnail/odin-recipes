Chicken Adobo Recipe

Description

Chicken Adobo is a popular Filipino dish made by simmering chicken in a savory mixture of soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, and spices. The chicken absorbs the rich flavors of the sauce while cooking, creating a tender and flavorful meal that pairs perfectly with steamed rice.

This dish is known for its balance of salty, tangy, and slightly savory flavors. Chicken Adobo is simple to prepare and uses common ingredients, making it a favorite home-cooked meal that is both comforting and delicious.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken, cut into pieces
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/2 cup vinegar
  • 4 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 teaspoon black peppercorns
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
  • 1 cup water

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a pan and sauté the garlic and onion until fragrant.
  2. Add the chicken pieces and cook until lightly browned.
  3. Pour in the soy sauce and water, then add bay leaves and peppercorns.
  4. Let it simmer for about 15 minutes.
  5. Add the vinegar and simmer for another 10–15 minutes until the chicken is tender.
  6. Taste the sauce and adjust seasoning if needed.
  7. Serve hot with steamed rice.
