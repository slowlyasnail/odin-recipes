Fried-chicken Recipe

Description

Fried chicken is a classic comfort food made by coating chicken pieces in seasoned flour and deep frying them until golden brown and crispy. The outside becomes crunchy while the inside stays juicy and flavorful.

This recipe is simple and perfect for beginners. It uses basic ingredients and results in crispy, delicious fried chicken that can be served with rice, mashed potatoes, or your favorite dipping sauce.

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken, cut into pieces
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • Oil for frying

Steps

  1. Soak the chicken pieces in buttermilk for at least 1 hour.
  2. Mix flour, salt, pepper, and paprika in a large bowl.
  3. Coat each chicken piece in the flour mixture.
  4. Heat oil in a deep pan to 350°F (175°C).
  5. Fry the chicken pieces in batches until golden brown and fully cooked.
  6. Drain on paper towels and serve hot.
